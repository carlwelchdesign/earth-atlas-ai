4. Finalize the decision, cost/access caveats, local backlog, Asana state, and architecture references.
5. Run `make check`, publish an unsigned commit and PR, require green CI, merge, and close EAT-014.

## Decision boundary

The spike may close with an **adjust** result even when a capability is unproven. It must not imply that static hosted PNG evidence is Media Set/Ontology-backed real imagery, that Developer Tier quotas are guaranteed, or that cleanup was tested. No live resource deletion, permission expansion, provider upload, AIP call, or public deployment is part of this closeout.
